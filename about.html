About EcoCycle

Our mission is a cleaner community with safer e-waste habits.

The problem we solve

Many households and small businesses keep dead batteries and broken electronics because municipal garbage collection skips e-waste and certified recycling centres are hard to find. EcoCycle is a digital solution that brings guidance, collection and rewards into one place.

Inform

Clear category guide with safe handling tips.

Connect

Drop-off locator and doorstep pickup booking.

Reward

Eco-credits and vouchers for every recycled gadget.

Frequently asked questions

Is my personal data safe on old phones?

Back up and factory-reset your device before handing it over.

Can I drop off a damaged battery?

Yes, but place it in a separate bag and tell the centre staff.

Is the pickup service free?

It is free for 5 or more items in this demo.
